# Pottery Coach field protocol

Status: preregistration draft; no recruitment may begin before privacy/ethics approval is recorded.

The study evaluates whether novice potters can set up and complete the controlled web prototype under real studio conditions. Participants must meet the studio’s wheel-safety prerequisites. The facilitator reads the bystander notice before capture begins and does not rescue unless safety or the protocol requires it. Product consent and optional media consent are separate.

Stop immediately for injury risk, participant withdrawal, a critical privacy/trust incident, thermal termination, or unexpected capture/storage of raw media. Pause cloud and proactive features after an incident and follow `incident-plan.md`.

The first cohort is 10 novices. Starting thresholds are at least 80% unassisted full-flow completion, at least 80% correct explanation of visual-only uncertainty, and zero privacy/trust critical incidents. Setup time and distraction are reported without post-hoc pass thresholds. Every enabled proactive cue requires independent approval from two experienced wheel instructors.
