# Field data dictionary

The participant pseudonym rotates and is not derived from contact information. Device records coarse class, OS, and browser; studio records light, backdrop, clay color, wetness, clutter, and wheel direction. Mount geometry is categorical. Completion distinguishes unassisted, rescued, incomplete, and stopped, with a nullable enumerated failure reason. Metrics reference versioned metric IDs. Spoken-query, barge-in, bystander-notice, deletion, and uncertainty-understanding outcomes are explicit booleans. Distraction uses a 1–5 response. A privacy/trust incident field is nullable but mandatory. Consent revisions and scheduled deletion date are mandatory. Raw media locations are excluded unless separate signed media consent exists.
