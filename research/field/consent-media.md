# Optional research media consent

This consent is optional and separate from using the prototype. It describes exactly which still images, audio, or video the research team proposes to record, why, who may access it, where it is stored, when it is deleted, and whether it may be shown to instructors. Refusing media does not affect participation or compensation.

No field is preselected. The participant may approve individual media types, revoke consent up to the stated deadline, and request deletion using the study contact. Blank or unsigned consent means no research media recording.
