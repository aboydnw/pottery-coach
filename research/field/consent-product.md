# Product study consent

Pottery Coach is an experimental web prototype, not safety equipment or a substitute for a studio instructor. It uses your device camera locally to estimate pottery dimensions. If you separately enable cloud voice, microphone audio is sent to the named provider. Raw continuous camera video is not uploaded or stored by the product.

Participation is voluntary. You may pause, skip a question, withdraw, or request deletion without losing compensation. The study records a pseudonym, device/studio conditions, task outcomes, timings, confidence-linked measurements, and your feedback. Contact, compensation, withdrawal deadline, deletion contact, and protocol revision must be completed by the study owner before recruitment.

Signature, date, participant pseudonym, protocol revision, compensation, study contact, privacy contact, and deletion deadline: **required before session**.
