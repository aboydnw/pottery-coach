# Pottery-domain review

This is an internal boundary review, not the required experienced-instructor sign-off.

## Findings

- A side silhouette supports external geometry and repeated visible motion; it does not support thickness, pressure, moisture, internal centering, compression, air-bubble, structural-safety, or causal technique claims.
- Centering/opening are especially ambiguous from the external contour. User-declared phase must outrank visual inference.
- “Press harder,” “use less water,” wheel-speed commands, and causal hand diagnoses could damage a piece and are prohibited until a cue-specific instructor approval and sensor basis exist.
- A useful cue must be actionable at that instant. Hand occlusion and rapid active shaping suppress proactive correction.
- Shrinkage is clay/body/process-specific; there is no silent universal default.

## Required external review

Two experienced wheel instructors independently rate every proactive cue against 8–12 clips for correctness, timing, actionability, possible damage, uncertainty, and distraction. Approve/conditional/reject plus disagreements are retained. Rejected/disputed cues stay disabled. Until then, proactive policy approval IDs remain null.

Domain scope is safe enough for descriptive prototype work; Gate 7 remains unpassed.
