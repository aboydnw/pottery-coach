# Coaching policy revision 1

Reactive questions may be answered only from an allow-listed tool result carrying an evidence ID, timestamp, confidence, and eligibility or explicit unmeasurable reason. Proactive policies remain disabled until they carry a two-instructor approval ID. Low confidence, stale evidence, active shaping, occlusion, user speech, duplicate evidence, or phase conflict produces silence and an audit reason.

Priority 1 is reserved for an approved urgent local cue and is the only priority allowed to interrupt; the local audio manifest is empty. Direct questions use priority 2. The global non-urgent cooldown is 20 seconds, the same-policy cooldown is 60 seconds, and encouragement is 180 seconds. These conservative defaults reduce repetition and distraction but remain hypotheses until the field study reports cues per minute and distraction.

Cloud phrasing receives immutable facts only. Numeric values must match eligible evidence within rounding tolerance. Unsupported causes or claims about pressure, moisture, wall/floor thickness, internal/perfect centering, or collapse are prohibited. A violation is audited and disables proactive cloud speech for the rest of the session; deterministic/reactive local behavior remains available.

Instructor decisions are recorded in [`coaching-policy-review.csv`](coaching-policy-review.csv). Disagreement means disabled or narrowed conditions, never implicit approval.
