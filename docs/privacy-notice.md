# Pottery Coach privacy notice

Revision: 2026-10-15

Pottery Coach is a controlled web prototype, run hands-free by voice. Camera frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored; a single reference picture of the empty wheel is kept in memory for the session to find the wheel head's edges and is discarded when the session ends. When a voice coach is configured for your device, your microphone audio is streamed to the named speech provider while the session runs, and the start screen says so before you tap Start. Without it, setup works on screen and no audio leaves the device.

The app may retain downsampled measurements, confidence, calibration references, events, cues, and diagnostics locally for up to 30 days. Transcript retention is off unless separately enabled. A still is stored only when you explicitly mark one, remains local unless upload consent is separately enabled, and is never inferred from ordinary camera use.

You can delete a session from its review screen. The receipt inventories local database, local blobs, caches, sync queue, backend, and any disclosed provider exception. Provider-side deletion is never claimed when no supported provider deletion route exists. Research media requires a separate optional consent and is not part of ordinary product use.
